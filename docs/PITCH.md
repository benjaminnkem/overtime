# Overtime — Pitch

## 60-Second Stage Pitch

> Every community WhatsApp group has the same energy — trivia debates, football arguments, "I bet I'd win." Nobody's turned that into an actual weekly event with real stakes.
>
> Overtime is a live trivia room for your group. A host schedules it, everyone puts in a small NIM entry fee, questions go live with a countdown, and the leaderboard re-ranks in real time as answers land. Top finishers get paid automatically when the session ends — not winner-take-all, so more people have a reason to come back next week.
>
> It's not a wager app and it's not a one-off prediction game — it's a room your group owns, week after week.
>
> [Live: join a session, answer a question, watch the leaderboard flip live, watch the payout fire at the end.]
>
> That's Overtime — appointment viewing, with a leaderboard.

## Anticipated Judge Questions + Prepared Answers

**"How is this different from Nimiq Pools or TeTe?"**
Both are single-session prediction or stakes tools. Overtime is a *room* a community owns — same group, same weekly slot, cumulative leaderboard across sessions, and payouts split across the top finishers rather than winner-take-all. The product is the recurring habit, not any one session's bet.

**"Isn't this just gambling with extra steps?"**
No — rank is determined by answer accuracy and speed, a skill measure, not chance. The competition's own rules explicitly permit skill-based games with clearly defined rules and prizes.

**"What happens if someone's connection lags and they lose points unfairly?"**
Each answer is timestamped server-side from the moment the question was broadcast, not by the client's own clock, and a small grace window absorbs normal network latency.

**"What's your real usage data?"**
[Fill in live: number of sessions run in the Room, unique participants, and evidence of repeat participants across weeks.]

**"What if too few people join a session?"**
A session needs a minimum number of entries to go live; below that, everyone's entry fee is refunded automatically rather than running an unfair session.

## The Demo Moment to Lead With
The live leaderboard flipping in real time as answers land — someone jumps from 4th to 1st mid-session while the room watches. That's the moment that makes it obviously not a static quiz app, and it's what should be on screen the instant judges are paying attention.
